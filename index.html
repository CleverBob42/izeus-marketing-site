iZeus Pty Ltd · in-house tool

Short videos of our own apps, published to our own YouTube and TikTok accounts

iZeus Marketing is the internal tool iZeus Pty Ltd uses to upload short gameplay and feature videos of its iPhone and iPad apps to the company's own YouTube channels and TikTok accounts, and to read how many people watched them. It is not offered to anyone outside the company.

Watch on YouTube

What it does

Uploads our videos

Each video is real recorded footage of one of our own apps, 15 to 30 seconds long, with captions and a voice-over. At most three a day per channel.

Reads our own numbers

It reads the view, like and comment counts of our own videos, so we can see which videos work.

Touches nothing else

It does not read other channels, viewers' details or comments, and it does not download or change anyone else's content.

How it works

  1. We record a short clip of one of our apps and add captions and a voice-over.
  2. A company staff member signs in once with Google and chooses the app's YouTube channel. The tool keeps only the access token Google returns, on the company's own computer.
  3. The tool uploads the finished video to that channel through the YouTube Data API, with a title and description we wrote.
  4. For TikTok, a staff member signs in once with TikTok on this site and approves iZeus Marketing for the app's TikTok account. The tool then posts the finished video to that account through the TikTok Content Posting API.
  5. Once a day it reads the view counts of our videos for our internal marketing report.

The tool uses YouTube API Services. See our Privacy Policy and Terms, the YouTube Terms of Service the Google Privacy Policy, and the TikTok Terms of Service.
