Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

This policy covers iZeus Marketing, an internal tool operated by iZeus Pty Ltd (ABN 56 643 023 750), South Brisbane, Australia (“we”, “us”). Our apps have their own privacy policies; this one is only about the tool.

YouTube API Services

iZeus Marketing uses YouTube API Services to upload videos to, and read statistics from, YouTube channels owned by iZeus Pty Ltd. By using iZeus Marketing you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy (www.google.com/policies/privacy).

TikTok

iZeus Marketing also uses the TikTok Login Kit and Content Posting API to publish videos to TikTok accounts owned by iZeus Pty Ltd (for example @whocanbeattrump). TikTok's handling of data is described in the TikTok Privacy Policy.

  • TikTok sign-in token. When a staff member connects one of our TikTok accounts, the tool receives an access token and refresh token with three permissions: read the account's basic profile (user.info.basic), and upload and publish videos (video.upload, video.publish).
  • Our account's open id and display name, to check every post goes to the right account, and the account's posting settings (allowed privacy levels, maximum video length) that TikTok requires us to read before posting.
  • The status of our own posts (processing, published or failed).

The tool does not read other TikTok users' profiles, videos, comments, followers or messages. Tokens are stored only on the company computer that runs the tool, as described below, and are never shared. Access can be revoked at any time in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions; we then delete the stored token within 7 days.

Meta (Facebook and Instagram)

iZeus Marketing also uses the Facebook Pages API and the Instagram API (Meta app “iZeus Marketing”) to publish videos as Reels to Facebook Pages and Instagram professional accounts owned by iZeus Pty Ltd (for example the Who Can Beat Trump 2028 Page and @whocanbeattrump). Meta's handling of data is described in the Meta Privacy Policy.

  • Page access token. When a staff member connects one of our Pages, the tool receives a Page access token with these permissions: list our Pages (pages_show_list), read our Page's posts and their statistics (pages_read_engagement, read_insights), publish to our Page (pages_manage_posts), and read and publish to our linked Instagram account (instagram_basic, instagram_content_publish, business_management), and read the views, reach and likes of our own Instagram posts (instagram_manage_insights).
  • Our Page's and Instagram account's id and name, to check every post goes to the right account.
  • The status and view counts of our own posts.

The tool does not read other people's profiles, posts, comments, followers or messages. Tokens are stored only on the company computer that runs the tool and are never shared. Access can be removed at any time in Facebook under Settings & privacy → Settings → Business integrations.

Deleting data held through Meta

The tool holds no data about members of the public. To ask us to delete the token and statistics we hold for one of our own accounts, or anything else we hold, contact us (below) or remove iZeus Marketing under Business integrations; we delete the stored data within 7 days and confirm by email.

Who uses the tool

Only iZeus Pty Ltd staff. The tool is not offered to the public, and only our own Google account, our own YouTube channels, TikTok accounts, Facebook Pages and Instagram accounts are ever connected to it.

What data the tool accesses

  • Google sign-in token. When a staff member signs in with Google, the tool receives an OAuth access token and refresh token for the chosen channel, with two permissions: upload videos (youtube.upload) and view the channel's account and videos (youtube.readonly).
  • Our channel's id and name, to check every upload goes to the right channel.
  • Statistics of our own videos: view, like and comment counts, and each video's id and privacy status.

The tool does not access other channels, other people's videos, comment text, subscriber lists, or any information about YouTube viewers.

How we use it

Only to upload our own videos and to show our own view counts in an internal marketing report. We do not use YouTube data for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

Where it is stored

The refresh token is stored on the company computer that runs the tool, in a file readable only by that computer's user account. Video statistics are stored in a local database on the same computer. Nothing is stored on servers we operate, and no cookies or tracking are used by the tool or this website.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share YouTube data with anyone. Video files and their titles and descriptions are sent only to YouTube, through the YouTube Data API.

Keeping and deleting data

  • Statistics are refreshed at least every 30 days and older copies are overwritten.
  • If access is revoked, the stored token stops working and we delete it, together with the stored statistics for that channel, within 7 days.
  • To ask us to delete stored data, contact us (below); we delete it within 7 days.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page: https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions. Choose “iZeus Marketing” and remove its access.

Changes

We will update this page if the tool's use of data changes, and change the date above.
